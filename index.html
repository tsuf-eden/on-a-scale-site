On a Scale

A cooperative party game for 3 to 10 players on one phone. Everyone gets a secret number from 1 to 100, describes it in terms of a topic, and the group tries to put everyone in order.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email tsuf.eden@gmail.com and include your phone model if something isn't working.

How to play

  1. Add 3 to 10 players and tap Start.
  2. Read the topic out loud. It has two ends, for example 1 = Silent and 100 = Deafening.
  3. Pass the phone around. Each player holds the card to see their secret number.
  4. Take turns saying something that fits your number. No numbers allowed!
  5. Together, drag the names onto the scale from lowest to highest.
  6. Reveal the numbers from the bottom and see how many were in the right place.

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet?
No. The game works fully offline and has no accounts.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Home → Settings. Sounds also follow your iPhone's silent switch.

Someone forgot their number.
On the Discuss screen, they tap their own name to see it again.

Can someone see another player's number?
Numbers only show on the Peek screen while that player holds the card, and they hide if the app goes to the background.

Where are my players saved?
Only on your phone. Deleting the app removes them.
