Privacy policy

On a Scale · Last updated 15 October 2026

In short: On a Scale does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

To remember things between games, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • the player names you type in, and their order and colours;
  • which topics were played recently, so they don't repeat too soon;
  • your settings (sound and vibration on or off).

The secret numbers dealt in a round are never saved. None of this information leaves your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising, and no third-party services that collect data.
  • No internet connection: the game works fully offline and does not send data anywhere. On Android, the app doesn't even request internet access.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
